Mission three

Use AI safely

The best AI explorers are curious, kind, and careful. These three superpowers help you use AI well.

🔒

Keep private things private

Do not share your full name, address, school, passwords, photos, or other personal information with an AI tool.

🔎

Check the answer

AI can make mistakes. Double-check important facts with a trusted adult, book, or reliable website.

💛

Be kind and fair

Use AI to learn and create—not to hurt, trick, or copy someone else's work without giving credit.

When should I ask a grown-up?

Ask a parent, teacher, or trusted grown-up before using a new AI tool, sharing anything personal, or believing an answer about health, safety, money, or other important topics.
