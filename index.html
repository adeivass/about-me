How I think

Engineering philosophy

Start with the problem

Technology is a means, not the outcome. I first clarify the user, business, and operational problem a system needs to solve.

Design for change

Architecture should make the next decision easier: clear boundaries, explicit trade-offs, and systems that can evolve in production.

Automate with intent

I use automation and AI to remove repetitive effort, improve decisions, and give engineering teams more time for meaningful problem solving.
