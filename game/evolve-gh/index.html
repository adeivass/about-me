📖 How to Play & Rules
  • 🗓️ Daily Seed: World temperature, rainfall, and initial placement generate automatically based on today's date. Everyone gets the same initial world.
  • ⌛ Tick Day: Advances time by 1 step, triggering growth, feeding, and starvation across the grid.
  • 🌱 Plants: Spread to adjacent empty tiles. Spread faster near 💧 Water or during HIGH rainfall.
  • 🐛 Herbivores: Eat adjacent 🌱 Plants to reproduce. Starve if no plants are nearby.
  • 🐺 Predators: Eat adjacent 🐛 Herbivores. Die out if prey is missing.
  • 🪨 Rocks: Serve as permanent, indestructible terrain barriers.
  • 🏆 Discoveries & Score: Meet special world conditions to unlock hidden discoveries worth +100 bonus points each.
