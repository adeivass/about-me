EVOLVE

A local, interactive ecosystem simulation
New here? How to play EVOLVE

Adapt your species

  1. Use + and − to distribute the three available adaptation points. Minus reverses only an uncommitted choice.
  2. Choose Commit Adaptation to apply your changes. Each commit begins another cycle with three points.
  3. Traits affect the organisms currently in the world. Population changes when you place organisms, or when they reproduce, starve, or are caught by predators.

Trait effects

  • Speed: increases movement speed.
  • Intelligence: organisms steer toward food when it is nearby.
  • Defense: reduces the chance of being caught by a predator.
  • Reproduction: increases the chance to reproduce when energy is high.

Interact with the world

  • Select ＋ Food, ＋ Predator, or ＋ Organism, then click the world to add it at that spot. Click Inspect to stop placing and inspect nearby objects.
  • Organisms spend energy over time, regain energy from food, and reproduce when they have enough energy. Food slowly regrows up to its cap.
  • Predators pursue organisms. A successful attack removes that organism; defense improves its survival chance.
  • Use Pause/Resume to stop or restart time. Paused worlds can still be edited.

Local simulation & saving

  • Blue: organisms. Green: food. Red: predators.
  • The counts and elapsed time show this browser's simulation only. There are no live players, global species counts, or server connection.
  • Your current world is saved in this browser and resumes from its saved state; it does not simulate while the page is closed.
  • Reset Game permanently clears this game's saved progress in this browser.

This is a simple game model, not a scientific ecological forecast. Hover or focus the controls for a quick explanation.
